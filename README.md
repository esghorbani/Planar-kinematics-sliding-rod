# Planar Kinematics Sliding Rod Teaching Demo

This repository contains the Beamer slide deck for a teaching demo on planar
rigid-body kinematics, prepared for a Mechanical Engineering Department job talk
at Kettering University.

The demo was built around one sliding-rod problem: a 5 m rigid rod has one end
constrained to slide down a vertical wall and the other end constrained to slide
along a horizontal floor. At the instant shown, the rod forms a 3-4-5 triangle,
point A is moving downward at 6 m/s, and point A has downward acceleration
5 m/s^2. The class goal is to find the motion of point B and the angular motion
of the rod.
